1 Fixtures & results

One matchday at a time — clean and premium. Pick a matchday below to see its fixtures, scores and player duels, then move to the next. The point table and top scorers are counted from these same results, exactly as in the main app. The semi-finals and final are at the bottom of this screen.

How scoring, duels & fixture cards work

Every team plays every other team once. Scores appear here as the organiser publishes them.

Player duels: when a match has player duels, the match is counted once in the point table — the team that wins more duels wins the match and earns 3 points (equal duels won = draw, 1 point each; loss = 0 points), however many duels were played.

📋 Fixture cards: inside each matchday there is a “Download Matchday fixture card (PNG)” button for that day only, plus the download-all button below for every matchday in order.

📋 Matchday fixture cards — one card for every matchday. Each card shows only that matchday's fixtures, ready to share before or after the matches are played.

2 Point table

Ranked by points, then goal difference, then goals scored. Win = 3 points, Draw = 1 point, Loss = 0. Points are counted from the player goals in the results, but one match always counts once: MP goes up by 1 per match, never per duel. If a match has player duels, the team that wins more duels wins that match (3 points), equal duels won is a draw (1 point each), and GF / GA are the total duel goals. A match with no duels played yet counts from its team score instead — the winning team gets 3 points, and a draw gives 1 point to both teams.

The table will appear here once the organiser publishes the teams.
